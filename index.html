About Marcos Roldan

Guidance rooted in healthcare, protection, and long-term planning.

Marcos Roldan is an independent Health and Life Insurance Broker with over 15 years of experience in healthcare, Medicare-focused insurance environments, client support, insurance education, and benefits planning.

Before building KLASSIC HEALTH, Marcos worked directly in healthcare operations and Medicare consulting, helping individuals understand coverage decisions, provider access, prescription concerns, plan structures, and how the wrong coverage can affect a family financially.

Today, Marcos helps clients build a structured coverage strategy that can evolve through different stages of life — from private health insurance under age 65, to Medicare planning, life insurance protection, supplemental coverage, small business benefits, and retirement strategies through Indexed Universal Life.

His approach is educational, transparent, and personalized. As an independent broker, Marcos is not limited to one insurance company. He compares multiple carriers and plan types to help clients choose coverage that aligns with their budget, doctors, prescriptions, family protection goals, and future retirement objectives.

KLASSIC HEALTH was created for clients who want more than a quick quote — they want a professional, premium, advisor-style experience that brings clarity to important insurance and financial protection decisions.
