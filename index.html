About Marcos Roldan

Guidance. Experience. Loyalty.

Marcos Roldan is an independent Health and Life Insurance Broker with over 15 years of experience in healthcare, insurance, Medicare consulting, client education, and benefits planning.

Before becoming an independent broker, Marcos worked in healthcare operations and Medicare-focused insurance environments, where he helped individuals and families understand their coverage, doctors, prescriptions, enrollment timelines, and financial protection options.

Today, through KLASSIC HEALTH, Marcos helps clients navigate every stage of life — from private health insurance under 65, to Medicare planning, life insurance protection, supplemental benefits, small business solutions, and retirement strategies using Indexed Universal Life.

As an independent broker, Marcos is not limited to one carrier. He compares multiple options to help create personalized strategies that align with each client’s health needs, budget, family protection goals, and long-term retirement objectives.

His mission is simple: Educate. Protect. Empower.
