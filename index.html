About Marcos Roldan

Guidance. Experience. Strategy.

Marcos Roldan is an independent Health and Life Insurance Broker with over 15 years of experience in healthcare, Medicare consulting, client education, insurance support, and benefits planning.

Before building KLASSIC HEALTH, Marcos worked in healthcare operations and Medicare-focused insurance environments, helping individuals and families understand their coverage, providers, prescriptions, enrollment timelines, and financial protection options.

Today, Marcos helps clients navigate every stage of life — from private health insurance under 65, to Medicare planning, life insurance protection, supplemental benefits, small business solutions, and retirement strategies using Indexed Universal Life.

As an independent broker, Marcos is not limited to one carrier. He compares multiple insurance options to help create personalized strategies that align with each client’s health needs, budget, family protection goals, and long-term retirement objectives.

His mission is simple: Educate. Protect. Empower.
