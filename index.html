Meet Marcos Roldan

Marcos Roldan is an independent Health and Life Insurance Broker with more than 15 years of experience in healthcare, insurance, client support, and benefits education.

Before becoming an independent broker, Marcos worked in healthcare operations and Medicare consulting, helping individuals understand how coverage decisions affect their health, finances, and family protection.

Today, Marcos helps clients compare Medicare, under-65 health insurance, life insurance, Indexed Universal Life, retirement protection strategies, and small business benefit solutions.

As an independent advisor, Marcos is not limited to one insurance company. His goal is to educate first, compare options clearly, and help each client choose coverage that fits their budget, lifestyle, and long-term goals.
